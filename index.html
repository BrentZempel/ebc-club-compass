About this pilot & what comes next

Source: EBC map brochure, 2025. Includes all 55 numbered listings across four levels. Names and map placement follow the brochure; current facilities, hours and availability have not been checked. A listing can cover multiple spaces, such as lounges or tennis courts.

How to try it: Choose “cycling,” a pool, or a locker room. Pick a starting location and compare the two levels. The green start label reflects your selection, not live tracking. “Copy pilot link” preserves your destination and start; it becomes shareable once this file is hosted.

For a member-ready version: Walk the most common routes with staff; name the stair and elevator banks; confirm accessible connections, controlled doors and seasonal areas; add landmark photos. Place location-specific QR codes at the entrance, elevator landings and major junctions so the starting point is already known.

Useful first test: Ask 5–10 new members to find a locker room, a studio and a pool. Record where they hesitate, which terms they search for, and where a photo or sign would help. This pilot sends no data and uses no AI service or member login.
